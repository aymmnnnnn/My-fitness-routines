My Fitness Routine Cards

Welcome to my daily fitness tracker. Here are the simple steps I follow each day to stay active and healthy.

Warm Up

Light movements to wake up your muscles and get your blood flowing easily.

Time: 5 minutes

Core Workout

Simple sit-ups and plank holds to keep your tummy muscles strong.

Time: 12 minutes

Leg Exercise

Easy lunges and body weight squats to strengthen your legs.

Time: 15 minutes

Jump Rope

Quick skipping activity to burn calories and boost your heart rate.

Time: 10 minutes

Cool Down

Slow breathing and gentle holds to help your body relax after exercise.

Time: 8 minutes

Healthy Snack

Eat an apple or a small fruit cup to regain your body energy.

Time: After workout

Daily Reminder: Stay active, drink water throughout the day, and get 8 hours of sleep.
